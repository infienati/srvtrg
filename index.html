How to use this roadmap

Follow these steps to find the programmes that fit your work. The sections below walk you through each one, and you can jump straight to matching programmes as you go.

    Step 1

    Service Functions and Areas of Work

    There are six Service Functions, each with its own Areas of Work. Depending on the Areas of Work you handle, you may refer to more than one Service Function. Select the ones that match your work.

    Step 2

    Functional competencies required for each Service Function

    The table shows the functional competencies you would need to perform your work in each Service Function. ✓* marks a critical competency, and ✓ marks a relevant one.

    Step 3

    Competency levels

    Each functional competency has proficiency levels. They map to these designations and indicative work areas. Use your designation to find your level, then see the programmes pegged at it.

    Case studies

    Three worked examples that follow the steps in the guide. Each one starts with a short story, answers the four questions, and ends with the programmes that match, taken live from the current catalogue.

    Competency & level matrix

    A reference view for HR and programme owners: every active programme against every functional competency, at a glance. Not intended for day-to-day browsing by learners, see the catalogue on the home page for that.

    🔍

    Scroll inside the table, up and down or sideways. The Programme and Modality columns, and the competency headings, stay in view.
